Justwhys_Hermes

Justwhys_Hermes is a private, single-user personal assistant app operated by its owner (justwhys@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

The app uses Google APIs (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Contacts) only to help the owner read, organize and manage the owner's own email, schedule and files, at the owner's request.
