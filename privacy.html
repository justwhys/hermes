Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Justwhys_Hermes ("the app") is a private personal assistant used only by its owner (justwhys@gmail.com).

Data accessed

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app may access the owner's Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests (for example, searching mail, summarizing messages, checking schedules, drafting replies). Data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with or transferred to third parties, except as necessary to process the owner's own requests with AI services chosen by the owner, and as required by law.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
